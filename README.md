> A curated list of tools, platforms, and resources for content creators.

Content creation spans video, audio, and written formats across many platforms. This list collects tools that help creators plan, produce, optimize, and grow their work — organized by category so you can find what's relevant to your workflow.

## Analytics

- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com) - Free web analytics platform, commonly used by creators with their own blogs or websites.
- [Later Analytics](https://later.com) - Social media analytics and scheduling with performance tracking across Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest.
- [SnapDataHub](https://snapdatahub.com) - Snapchat analytics tool for public profile, Spotlight and engagement metrics.
- [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com) - Tracks public statistics and growth trends for YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, and other platforms.
- [Streamlabs Analytics](https://streamlabs.com) - Analytics and dashboard tools for live streamers on Twitch and YouTube.
- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com) - Browser extension offering YouTube channel management, keyword research, A/B testing, and performance analytics.
- [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com) - Browser extension and dashboard providing YouTube channel analytics, keyword scoring, and competitor tracking.

## SEO

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - SEO toolset for keyword research, backlink analysis, and content gap analysis, usable for blog and video metadata.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) - Visualizes common search questions and phrases around a given topic or keyword.
- [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) - Free keyword research tool from Google Ads, useful for search-volume estimates.
- [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - Keyword and content idea generator with basic SEO metrics.

## Video Editing

- [Adobe Premiere Pro](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) - Industry-standard subscription-based video editing software.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) - Free cross-platform video editor popular for short-form vertical video.
- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) - Free, professional-grade video editing and color-grading software.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - Editor that lets you edit video and audio by editing a text transcript.
- [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) - Free, open-source tool for converting and compressing video files.
- [OrkasVideoStudio](https://github.com/Orkas-AI/Orkas-VideoStudio) - Local-first toolkit for agent-driven video composition, editing, transcription, and captions.
- [Shotcut](https://shotcut.org) - Free, open-source cross-platform video editor.

## Content Planning

- [Airtable](https://airtable.com) - Spreadsheet-database hybrid used for tracking content ideas, sponsorships, and publishing schedules.
- [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) - Widely used for scheduling recording, editing, and publishing deadlines.
- [Milanote](https://milanote.com) - Visual board tool for organizing ideas, scripts, and mood boards.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so) - Flexible workspace commonly used by creators for content calendars and script drafting.
- [Trello](https://trello.com) - Kanban-style board tool used for planning content pipelines and production stages.

## Community Tools

- [Circle](https://circle.so) - Community platform for hosting discussions, courses, and events outside of social media.
- [Discord](https://discord.com) - Platform for building and moderating creator communities via text, voice, and video channels.
- [Discourse](https://www.discourse.org) - Open-source forum software for building long-form community discussion spaces.
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) - Membership platform allowing creators to offer paid tiers and exclusive content to supporters.
- [StreamElements](https://streamelements.com) - Chatbot, alerts, and community engagement tools for live streamers.

## Learning Resources

- [Google's Primer](https://www.yourprimer.com) - Free bite-sized business and marketing lessons relevant to creator entrepreneurship.
- [r/NewTubers](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/) - Community subreddit for creators sharing advice and feedback.
- [Skillshare Creator Classes](https://www.skillshare.com) - Subscription platform with courses on video editing, content strategy, and design.
- [Think Media (YouTube channel)](https://www.youtube.com/@ThinkMediaTV) - Long-running channel covering video gear, YouTube strategy, and creator business advice.
- [YouTube Creator Academy](https://creatoracademy.youtube.com) - Official YouTube courses covering channel growth, monetization, and production.

## Promotion & Advertising

- [Google Ads](https://ads.google.com) - Advertising platform for running paid video, display, and search campaigns across YouTube, Google Search, and partner sites.
- [Meta Ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads) - Advertising platform for running paid campaigns across Facebook and Instagram, including in-feed and Reels placements.
- [Promolta](https://www.promolta.com) - Paid distribution service that promotes YouTube videos across a network of third-party publishers to drive views and subscribers. 
- [Spotify Ad Studio](https://ads.spotify.com) - Self-serve platform for creating and running paid audio and video ad campaigns on Spotify.
- [TikTok Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com) - Advertising platform for running paid in-feed and branded campaigns to promote content and accounts on TikTok.
